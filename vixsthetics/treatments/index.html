Neurotoxin

Jeuveau, Xeomin, and Daxxify are each FDA approved for the temporary improvement of frown lines between the brows in adults. Other areas are discussed at your consultation.

  • Neurotoxin treatments

    $11 per unit

    Jeuveau, Xeomin, or Daxxify to soften lines that form with expression, chosen for you at consultation. Many people search for this as Botox.

  • Full face neurotoxin treatmentSignature service

    $11 per unit

    A wrinkle relaxer treatment planned across the face as one plan rather than one area at a time, with Daxxify as the featured product.

    This is an off label use of an FDA approved product; your provider will discuss whether it is appropriate for you.

  • Lip flip

    $200

    A small amount of neurotoxin relaxes the muscle around the mouth so the upper lip turns gently outward, without adding volume.

    This is an off label use of an FDA approved product; your provider will discuss whether it is appropriate for you.
