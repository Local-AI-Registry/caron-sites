What happens at the consultation

  1. Book online or call

    Choose a time on the practice’s Jane booking page, or call (631) 408-8376.

  2. Skin and facial analysis

    Victoria looks at your face as a whole: structure, skin, and how it moves.

  3. Your goals and medical history

    You talk through what you would like to change and what you would rather keep. Your history decides what is safe for you.

  4. Your options, with cost and downside

    Victoria explains the treatments that suit you, what each costs in full, and what can go wrong.

  5. A timeline for your plan

    You leave with a plan and a timeline, and no pressure to start that day.
