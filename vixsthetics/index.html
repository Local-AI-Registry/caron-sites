Quick answers before you book

Who performs treatments at Vixsthetics?

Victoria Yunaev, a physician assistant, performs every treatment at Vixsthetics. Dr. Peggy Bastien, a physician board certified in obstetrics and gynecology, is the medical director and supervising physician. Medical services are provided by Bastien Medical PLLC.

Meet Victoria or read about medical oversight.

Where is Vixsthetics?

Vixsthetics is at 1149 Merrick Ave Ste 17, Merrick, NY 11566, on the South Shore of Long Island. Call (631) 408-8376 or see directions.

Which neurotoxins does Vixsthetics use?

Three: Jeuveau, Xeomin, and Daxxify. Many people call the whole category Botox. Victoria chooses the product for your plan at your consultation, and Daxxify is the featured product for the full face neurotoxin treatment.

How much does neurotoxin treatment cost?

Neurotoxin is priced at $11 per unit. The number of units depends on the areas treated and is set with you at your consultation, so you know the full cost before treatment. See pricing for every treatment.

What happens at a consultation?

The consultation is a 25 minute visit with Victoria. It includes a skin and facial analysis, a review of your goals and medical history, a discussion of the treatment options that suit you, and a timeline for your plan. How booking works.

How do I book?

Book online through the practice’s Jane booking page, or call (631) 408-8376.
