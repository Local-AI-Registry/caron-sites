Pricing and financing

Prices are printed openly, by unit and by plan. You know the full cost before treatment.

How pricing works

Neurotoxin is priced by the unit, because the amount you need depends on the areas treated and how strong those muscles are. Filler is priced by the syringe. At your consultation Victoria sets out the full plan and its total cost before anything is scheduled, so there are no surprises on the day.

A full face plan is priced the same way: the units for each area added together. A plan that uses fewer units costs less, and you are never asked to commit to more than you want.
