Medical oversight at Vixsthetics

Dr. Peggy Bastien is the medical director at Vixsthetics. She supervises every treatment.

Dr. Peggy Bastien, medical director

Dr. Bastien graduated magna cum laude with a BS in nursing and biology and pre medicine from Molloy University. She earned her Doctorate of Medicine, then completed her internship and chief residency in obstetrics and gynecology at SUNY Upstate Medical University hospital in Syracuse, NY.

She is board certified in obstetrics and gynecology by the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology, holds academic and clinical affiliations with medical centers in New York and New Jersey, and is licensed in multiple states.

Dr. Bastien continued her education with training in aesthetic medicine, with an emphasis on minimally invasive gynecologic and cosmetic procedures, including neuromodulators, PRP, PDO threads, laser treatments, cryotherapy, and microneedling. She is also trained in weight loss treatments, IV vitamin therapy, hormone replacement, and sexual health.
