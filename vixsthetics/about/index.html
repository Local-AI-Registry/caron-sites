About the practice

Vixsthetics in Merrick, NY: injectables by physician assistant Victoria Yunaev, including neurotoxin, fillers, under eye and biostimulators, for natural results.

How Vixsthetics began

Vixsthetics was founded from a deep passion for a holistic, patient centered approach, built on the belief that thorough care and compassion belong in one welcoming space. Victoria Yunaev worked in a cardiac intensive care unit for three years, then turned to her passion, medical aesthetics, to help people feel more confident in themselves.

The approach: restraint and balance

Victoria specializes in natural enhancement: never changing the way you look, only refining the features you already have. Treatment is planned across the whole face, so a change in one area supports the others, and nothing is added for its own sake.

Every patient is treated with compassion and understanding. Your comfort, happiness, and confidence come first, and each visit is personal and tailored to you.

Who you will see

Victoria Yunaev, a physician assistant, performs every treatment. Dr. Peggy Bastien is the medical director and supervising physician. Medical services are provided by Bastien Medical PLLC.
